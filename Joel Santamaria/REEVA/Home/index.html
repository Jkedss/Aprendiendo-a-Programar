How It Works

A process designed to close the fashion loop and give new life to every garment.

  1. 01

    Donation

    Send your forgotten clothes to our collection centers to start your new story.

  2. 02

    Transformation

    Emerging designers reimagine each garment using artisanal upcycling techniques.

  3. 03

    Post

    Each piece is documented with its story and shared on our digital platform.

  4. 04

    Purchase

    Find your new piece with a story and support our sustainable mission.
